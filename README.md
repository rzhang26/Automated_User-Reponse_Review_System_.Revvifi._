
Application for Automating Analysis and Review of User-Reponse Data for *[Revvifi]([url](https://revvifi.org/))*

Description of the application (README): This application, which I’ve dubbed the Revvifi Automated User-Response Feedback Analysis Application, will take in google form data from weekly activity report submissions, evaluate its validity based on any plausible provided proof, apply the scoring guidelines and output a row of data displaying an individual’s name, engagement level, weekly activities, and presence / absence of a strike. In accordance with the scoring guidelines from the Table of Ranks, strikes are assigned to individuals who have displayed no effort or have provided insufficient proof to substantiate their claims. When a “striked submission” submission is processed, an instant notification will also be sent to the chapter supervisor via discord for them to take immediate action in regards to notifying the striked individual of their lack of activity and take any necessary actions.
